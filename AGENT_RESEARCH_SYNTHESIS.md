# AGENT_RESEARCH_SYNTHESIS

Updated: 2026-09-29

## Preserved trajectory

### Hypothesis: the developmental cell ecology should become the whole chatbot
**Evidence/test:** Agent v0/v1 established useful causal recurrent mechanisms and localized destructive learning, but did not establish broad language generation.
**Result:** failed as a default architectural assumption.
**Revision:** use developmental machinery where it earns a role; do not require it to be the language substrate.

### Hypothesis: avoid Transformers / attention
**Evidence/test:** literature and project review showed attention remains unusually strong for precise relational access.
**Result:** rejected as an architectural rule.
**Revision:** Transformer/attention is an experimental variable; persistent state and attention may be complementary.

### Hypothesis: broad conversation requires broad factual knowledge in the generator parameters
**Evidence/test:** retrieval-augmented language modeling shows external addressable knowledge can carry substantial factual burden.
**Result:** provisionally settled against the strong form.
**Revision:** separate semantic/world memory from language capability. The generator must learn how to retrieve/use/reject evidence, not memorize all evidence.

### Hypothesis: fixed tokenization is necessary for an efficient language spine
**Evidence/test:** ByT5/CANINE establish token-free feasibility; MEGABYTE and BLT show that multiscale byte processing can recover efficiency and competitive quality.
**Result:** provisionally settled against necessity.
**Revision:** raw bytes/characters are a viable external interface, but naive one-byte-one-global-step processing is a poor default. Learned/local patching is the stronger family.

### Hypothesis: persistent recurrent/SSM state can replace attention wholesale
**Evidence/test:** Mamba and RWKV establish strong recurrent language modeling and attractive inference economics. Controlled Mamba comparisons show weaknesses in copying, in-context learning, and long-context reasoning; Mamba-2 hybrids recover these deficits and can outperform matched Transformers.
**Result:** weakened.
**Revision:** recurrent compression is best treated as cheap continuity, while a limited precise-access path remains a strong candidate for current/retrieved evidence.

### Hypothesis: pure recurrence/SSM vs hybrid precise-access spine is still the main architecture-family question
**Evidence/test:** Griffin/Hawk provide evidence down to roughly 100M parameters that recurrence plus local attention can beat matched Transformer baselines on language modeling while retaining recurrent inference advantages. MQAR/Zoology and mechanistic associative-recall studies show that aggregate language loss can conceal serious exact-recall deficits in efficient recurrent/convolutional models. Taipan independently combines Mamba-2 with selective attention for retrieval-heavy contexts, and Nemotron-H corroborates the broader hybrid pattern at larger scale.
**Result:** provisionally settled at the architecture-family level against pure recurrence as the default.
**Revision:** stop spending research cycles asking whether the first serious conversational spine should be pure SSM/recurrent versus hybrid. The better-supported default family is bounded recurrent/SSM state plus limited precise access. The unresolved architecture details—cell choice, attention budget/type, patching, and sub-100M crossover—remain engineering/experimental variables rather than the highest-leverage research frontier.

## Current synthesis
The evidence now supports a division of labor across information-access regimes:

1. **Raw text boundary:** bytes/characters preserve exact text and avoid dependence on a fixed external tokenizer.
2. **Local composition / patching:** compress fine-grained byte spans before expensive global processing.
3. **Persistent recurrent/SSM state:** cheaply carry conversational continuity and slow context.
4. **Limited precise-access path:** local, sparse, selective, or occasional attention provides direct access to retrieved passages, names, code, current-turn evidence, and other information that should not be forced through lossy state compression.
5. **External semantic/world memory:** broad factual knowledge lives outside the trainable generator.
6. **Homegrown generator:** every response is still generated by our own learned machinery; retrieval supplies evidence rather than hidden intelligence.

The architecture-family question has therefore crossed the threshold from research bottleneck to provisional engineering prior. The first serious whole-agent prototype should not be pure recurrence by default, but neither does the evidence justify dense global attention everywhere.

## Current question status
- “Must we use fixed subword tokens?” — **provisionally settled: no.**
- “Must broad factual knowledge live in the generator parameters?” — **provisionally settled: no.**
- “Should the first evidence-grounded conversational spine default to pure recurrence/SSM or a hybrid with precise access?” — **provisionally settled in favor of the hybrid family.**
- Exact tiny-scale architecture details remain open, but are no longer the highest-leverage literature question.

## Single highest-value next research question
> For a small, from-scratch hybrid conversational spine, which training objective and curriculum most efficiently produces a broad productive semantic interface rather than merely low next-step prediction loss?

The next cycle should compare independent training paths such as:
- plain causal prediction;
- dialogue-conditioned causal prediction;
- denoising/bidirectional auxiliary objectives;
- retrieval-conditioned generation;
- staged curricula that move from general sequence modeling into dialogue, evidence use, and memory-conditioned generation.

The decision criterion should be whole-agent conversational capability: free generation, topic switching, paraphrase/semantic transfer, retrieval-grounded answering, multi-turn reference control, and robustness to distractors—not validation loss alone.

## Cheapest discriminating experiment worth running later
After the training-objective literature is synthesized, hold architecture mostly fixed and train a small number of matched variants of one tiny hybrid spine under the strongest candidate curricula.

Evaluate:
- free conversational continuation across unrelated topics;
- semantic/paraphrase generalization;
- exact use of an injected retrieved passage;
- retrieval with irrelevant/conflicting distractors;
- multi-turn entity/reference retention;
- next-byte/patch loss;
- training compute, convergence speed, RAM, and inference latency.

Do not tune the architecture between curriculum variants. The purpose is to test whether training objective/curriculum creates a large whole-agent gain before spending more cycles on architectural refinement.
